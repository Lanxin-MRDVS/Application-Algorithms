## Product Overview

The MRDVS M series is a family of RGB-D depth cameras based on the Sony iToF sensor, delivering high-performance depth data from 0.2 m to 5 m with spatially and temporally aligned RGB and depth output for object recognition in diverse scenarios. The M series includes M4 Mega, M4, M4Pro, M4Pro 850, and M4Pro Wfov variants.

MRDVS also offers industrial solutions built on the M series depth cameras, including [Intelligent Forklift Pallet Recognition](https://hub.mrdvs.cn/doc/editor/019cb6ff-39ed-77dc-9026-c2b7e39dfd11), [AGV Forklift Bin Stacking](https://hub.mrdvs.cn/doc/editor/019cb762-f626-77e0-8600-24d123d9d982), [3D Vision-Guided Soft-Package Depalletizing](https://hub.mrdvs.cn/doc/editor/019cb76f-36a8-7a1d-aaf3-f5b467152165), and [3D Vision-Based Volume Measurement](https://hub.mrdvs.cn/doc/editor/019cb771-e5e9-778b-8472-a37c2bbb339a), enabling faster integration for industry customers.

## Key Features

### 1. RGB-D Fusion for Enhanced Recognition Accuracy

By fusing color and texture information from the RGB image with depth data (ToF), the M series cameras improve object detection accuracy and robustness, compensating for the limitations of single-modality sensing. In low-light, complex-background, or low-contrast scenarios, depth data provides critical geometric information for accurate object identification and localization.

### 2. High-Performance Real-Time Imaging

The series supports high-speed data acquisition at 15–25 fps, ensuring precise measurement in dynamic environments such as conveyor-belt package inspection and robotic-arm pick-and-place operations.

### 3. Adaptability to Complex Lighting Conditions

Equipped with **HDR (High Dynamic Range)**, the cameras capture both high-reflectivity and low-reflectivity objects simultaneously, maintaining stable operation under strong light or outdoor conditions.

### 4. Multi-Platform Support and Development Flexibility

Supporting **C/C++, ROS, and Python**, the cameras are compatible with Linux, Windows, and ARM Linux platforms. Rich tools and APIs are provided for developers, suitable for applications such as autonomous robots, AGV material handling, and warehouse monitoring.
